# Framework for the National Waste Management Regulatory Body (NWMU)
**Strategic Lead:** Ahmed Ismail Saed Hussein  
**Agency:** Ministry of Environment and Climate Change, JSL.

## 🏛️ Executive Proposal
A structural proposal for establishing the National Waste Management Unit. This document outlines the regulatory protocols, coordination mechanisms between local government and national agencies, and climate-resilient monitoring systems.
